# FarmMotion 0.4.2 public beta

Ever wish farm simulator had force feedback on steering wheels? This mod fixes that. Feel every bump in the road and field, and customize how strong you want to feel it.

## Downloads

- **[Windows app](https://github.com/wesleygarnett/FarmMotion/releases/download/v0.4.2/FarmMotion-v0.4.2-win-x64.zip)**
- **[FS25 mod](https://github.com/wesleygarnett/FarmMotion/releases/download/v0.4.2/FS25_FarmMotionTelemetry.zip)**

## Install

1. Extract the app ZIP and open **FarmMotion.exe**.
2. With FS25 closed, copy the mod ZIP into **Documents/My Games/FarmingSimulator2025/mods**. Don't extract it.
3. Start FS25 and enable **FarmMotion Telemetry** in your save.
